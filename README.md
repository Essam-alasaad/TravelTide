# TravelTide Customer Segmentation

## Project Overview
TravelTide, launched in 2021, is a fast-growing e-booking platform. To improve customer retention, the company plans to implement a personalized rewards program. This project aims to provide data-driven insights to identify key customer segments and tailor relevant perks to improve retention and engagement.

## Objective
- Provide data-driven insights to identify key customer segments  
- Tailor relevant perks to improve retention and engagement

## Key Insights
- Family Travelers, Young Adult Frequent Travelers, and Dreamers account for most flight and hotel bookings as well as checked luggage.  
- These segments also show the longest lead times before booking.  
- Business Travelers are the highest spenders per person, prioritizing flexibility and comfort.  
- The US is TravelTide’s main market, with promising growth opportunities in Canada.  
- Growth potential exists in segments like Young Frequent Travelers and Senior Travelers, where the right perks can boost conversions and bookings.
